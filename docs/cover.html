Who qualifies for VA health care?
Veterans who served on active duty and didn’t receive a dishonorable discharge may be eligible 1. Reserve and Guard members may qualify if called to active duty by federal order 2.
1  VA Health Care — Eligibilityva.gov · verbatim source
